4Enable Email Confirmation

Go back to Auth Settings and enable email confirmation:

🔗 Open Auth Settings
Navigate to: Authentication → Providers → Email

Make sure these are enabled (✅):
  • Enable email provider
  • Enable email confirmations (This is the important one!)
  • Secure email change
✅ Click "Save" after enabling.

5Test Your Setup

Now test that emails are being sent:

⚠️ Use a REAL email address you can access!
You need to receive the email to test the confirmation link.
🧪 Open Test Page 🚀 Test on Live Site
Testing Steps:
  1. Enter your real email address
  2. Click "Test Signup" or "Create Account"
  3. Check your email inbox (and spam folder!)
  4. Click the confirmation link in the email
  5. You should be logged in!

6Verify in Supabase Logs

Check if emails are being sent successfully:

📊 Open Auth Logs
Look for:
  • ✅ "email sent successfully"
  • ✅ "confirmation email sent"
  • ❌ SMTP connection errors (if any)
⚠️ If you see errors:
  • Double-check SMTP credentials
  • Verify password is correct (Laur197511$)
  • Try port 465 instead of 587
  • Check Bluehost cPanel if email account is active

🎉 Setup Complete!

You've configured:

  • ✅ SMTP settings with Bluehost

  • ✅ Beautiful email templates
  • ✅ Email confirmation enabled

Your signup flow is now fully functional with email confirmation!

💡 Need Help?
If emails aren't arriving:
  • Check spam folder
  • Wait 60 seconds between tests (rate limit)
  • Verify SMTP credentials in Bluehost cPanel
  • Check Supabase Auth Logs for error messages
