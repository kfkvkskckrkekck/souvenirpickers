# Collector Features Guide

## Collections Feature

### How It Works
Collections allow collectors to organize and save listings and desires into curated groups.

### How to Use Collections

#### Creating a Collection
1. Navigate to **Collections** in the collector's menu
2. Click **"New Collection"** button
3. Enter a collection name (e.g., "European Souvenirs", "Gift Ideas")
4. Add an optional description
5. Choose whether to make it public or private
6. Click **"Create Collection"**

#### Adding Listings to Collections
1. Browse listings in the **Listings** view
2. When you find a listing you like, click the **pink "Save" button** (with heart icon)
3. A modal will appear showing your collections
4. Click on the collection you want to add the listing to
5. Optionally add notes about why you're saving it
6. The listing is now saved in your collection!

#### Managing Collections
- View all your collections in the Collections page
- Click on a collection to see all items saved in it
- Remove items from collections by clicking the "Remove from Collection" button
- Toggle collections between public and private using the eye icon
- Delete entire collections using the trash icon

### Collection Use Cases
- **Dream Destinations**: Save listings from places you want to visit
- **Gift Ideas**: Organize potential gifts for friends and family
- **Cultural Treasures**: Collect unique cultural items from around the world
- **Budget Planning**: Group items by price range
- **Event Planning**: Collections for specific occasions

## Matched Pickers Feature

### How It Works
The Matched Pickers feature uses a smart algorithm to find pickers who can fulfill your desires based on:
- **Distance (40 points)**: Closer pickers score higher
- **Rating (30 points)**: Higher-rated pickers get more points
- **Experience (20 points)**: More completed orders = better score
- **Activity (10 points)**: Recent activity boosts the score

### How to Get Matched with Pickers

#### Step 1: Create a Desire with GPS Coordinates
1. Go to **Client Desires** in the sidebar
2. Click **"Create New Desire"**
3. Fill in what you want (title, description)
4. **IMPORTANT**: Add a specific location with GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude)
   - You can use the location picker
   - Or enter coordinates manually
5. Set your budget and other details
6. Click **"Create Desire"**

#### Step 2: View Your Matches
1. Navigate to **Matched Pickers** in the collector's menu
2. The system automatically calculates matches for all your desires
3. You'll see pickers ranked by match score (0-100)
4. Match quality indicators:
   - **80-100**: Excellent Match (green)
   - **60-79**: Good Match (blue)
   - **40-59**: Fair Match (yellow)
   - **0-39**: Possible Match (gray)

#### Step 3: Contact Matched Pickers
- Click **"View All Pickers"** to see the picker's full profile
- Message them directly about your desire
- Check their ratings, reviews, and location

### Why You Might Not See Matches
- Your desires need GPS coordinates (latitude/longitude)
- No active pickers within 500km of your desired location
- Only pickers active in the last 90 days are included
- The picker must have location data set

### Tips for Better Matches
- Create desires for popular tourist destinations
- Be specific with your location coordinates
- Check back regularly as more pickers join the platform

## Trending Destinations Feature

### How It Works
Trending Destinations shows the hottest souvenir locations based on real-time platform activity from the last 90 days.

### Trend Score Calculation
Each destination gets points based on:
- **Active Listings**: 10 points each
- **Collector Desires**: 15 points each (shows demand)
- **Active Pickers**: 5 points each (shows supply)
- **Completed Orders**: 20 points each (proven demand)

### Trend Badges
- **On Fire**: 500+ points (red badge)
- **Very Hot**: 300-499 points (orange badge)
- **Hot**: 150-299 points (yellow badge)
- **Trending**: 0-149 points (blue badge)

### How to Use Trending Destinations
1. Navigate to **Trending Destinations** in the collector's menu
2. Browse the ranked list of destinations
3. Click **"Explore [Location]"** to see all listings from that destination
4. Click **"Refresh"** to update the trending data
5. Use trending data to:
   - Discover popular souvenir locations
   - See where pickers are most active
   - Find locations with high demand
   - Plan your souvenir collection strategy

### Why You Might Not See Trending Destinations
- The platform is new and building up activity
- Not enough pickers have created listings
- Not enough collectors have created desires
- No recent orders have been completed

### How to Help Build Trending Data
- Create desires for locations you're interested in
- Browse and purchase from existing listings
- If you're a picker, add listings for your location
- Complete orders to generate activity data
